Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This policy explains what personal information Renoriser Inc. ("Renoriser," "we," "us"), a renovation company based in Hamilton, Ontario, collects through this website, how we use it, and your rights under Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

Who is responsible

Renoriser Inc. is responsible for the personal information it collects. Our Privacy Officer is accountable for our compliance with this policy and can be reached at info@renoriser.ca (subject: "Privacy Officer") or (365) 476-9000.

What we collect

Information you give us. When you submit our quote form, or email or call us, we collect what you provide: typically your name, email address, phone number, project type, and any project details you choose to share. You don't need an account to use this site.

Advertising cookies. This site uses a Google Ads tag. It sets cookies and uses identifiers to measure how well our ads work, for example whether a visit from one of our ads led to a quote request. When you submit the quote form, we tell Google that a request was sent and which project type was chosen; we do not send your name, email or phone number to Google. Google may also use this information to show you Renoriser ads on other sites and apps. This data is linked to your browser or device, so we don't describe it as anonymous. We don't use Google Analytics.

Server logs. Like any website, our hosting provider automatically records technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to deliver and secure the site.

How we use it

  • To respond to your quote request or inquiry and follow up about your project;
  • To prepare and send you an estimate, and to carry out and support any project you hire us for;
  • To measure our advertising and improve our website.

We use your information only for the purposes you'd reasonably expect when contacting a renovation company. We do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone.

Who we share it with

We share personal information only with service providers that help us run the website and the business, and only as needed for the purposes above:

  • Web3Forms, which delivers quote-form submissions to our inbox;
  • Zoho Mail, our email provider, which stores the messages we send and receive;
  • Google, which runs the Google Ads tag described above;
  • Netlify, our website host, which serves the site and keeps its server logs.

We may also disclose information where the law requires it.

Storage outside Canada

Some of these providers store or process information outside Canada, including in the United States. While it is there, it is subject to the laws of that country and may be accessible to its courts, law-enforcement and national-security authorities.

How long we keep it

  • Inquiries that don't become projects are deleted after 24 months.
  • Records of projects we carry out (contracts, estimates, invoices and related messages) are kept for 7 years, for tax and warranty purposes.

Your rights

You may ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, and you may withdraw your consent at any time, subject to legal or contractual limits (for example, records we must keep for tax purposes). Contact our Privacy Officer using the details above; we'll respond within 30 days.

If you're not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Cookies and your choices

The first time you visit, a notice explains our advertising cookie. You can turn ad measurement off there, or at any time with the "Ad preferences" link at the bottom of every page. Once it's off, the tag stops collecting advertising data on the page you're on, and this site no longer loads it on later pages. Cookies Google set before you opted out stay in your browser until you clear them. Your choice is saved in your browser, so you'll need to set it again if you clear your browser data or use another device.

You can also control how Google personalizes ads at Google's My Ad Center, or block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site works normally either way.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your information? Reach us at info@renoriser.ca or (365) 476-9000.
